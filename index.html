Project Management Software for Small Teams & Startups

Paper5 is the centralized team task management and sprint execution platform. Organize daily tasks, automate sprint tracking with GitHub sync, and maintain team accountability without coordination drag.

Why Small Teams Choose Paper5

  • Team Task Management Software: Plan, assign, and track tasks with strict single ownership to ensure accountability.
  • Real-Time GitHub Synchronization: Automate ticket progression as pull requests are opened, reviewed, and merged.
  • Lightweight Sprint Planning: Protect sprint scope and eliminate endless status meetings with asynchronous velocity insights.
  • Collaborative Documentation: Keep meeting notes, project specs, and sprint retrospectives directly beside execution boards.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is project management software for small teams?

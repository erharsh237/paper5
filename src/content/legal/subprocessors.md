# Subprocessors List

**Last Updated:** August 2026

To provide the SprintOS service, Paper5 engages the following third-party entities to process personal data on our behalf ("Subprocessors"). 

We require all Subprocessors to adhere to strict data protection and security standards.

## Core Infrastructure & Hosting
- **Google LLC (Google Cloud Platform & Firebase)**
  - **Purpose:** Cloud hosting, database (Firestore), authentication (Firebase Auth), serverless functions (Cloud Functions), and file storage.
  - **Location:** Global (Primarily US-Central for Cloud Functions).

## Payment Processing
- **Stripe, Inc.**
  - **Purpose:** Secure processing of subscription payments, invoicing, and customer billing management.
  - **Location:** United States / Global.

## Optional Third-Party Integrations
If a Workspace Owner or Member explicitly connects a third-party service, data may be processed by these entities according to their respective privacy policies. We only transmit data necessary to facilitate the integration (e.g., sending a webhook message).

- **GitHub, Inc.** (If connected for PR/Commit tracking)
- **Google LLC** (If Google Calendar is connected)
- **Discord Inc.** (If webhook notifications are enabled)
- **Slack Technologies, LLC** (If webhook notifications are enabled)
- **Vercel Inc.** (If connected for deployment tracking)

## Updates to this List
We may update this list as our business needs change. We will provide notice of any new Subprocessors by updating this page.
